02 📊 Business Analytics

NexaMart Retail Sales Dashboard

🎯 Objective

Analyze retail sales performance to identify customer behaviour patterns, top-performing regions, product category revenue, and channel effectiveness — enabling data-driven decisions across marketing, inventory, and operations.

📌 Business Problem

NexaMart lacked consolidated visibility into which segments, regions, and channels were driving revenue, making it difficult to prioritize spend, optimize inventory, and improve fulfilment processes at scale.

💰 ₹21.18M Total Sales
📦 31.05K Total Orders
✅ 92.25% Success Rate

📈 Key Business Insights

  • Women contributed ~64% of total sales revenue
  • Maharashtra led all states with ₹3.0M in revenue
  • Sales peaked in March, declining steadily through Q4
  • Set category generated the highest product revenue (₹10.5M)
  • Amazon drove the highest order volume across all channels
